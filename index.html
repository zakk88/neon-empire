TAKE WHAT’S YOURS

Power moves in three directions

The city rewards the ruthless and buries the slow. Choose how your empire grows—block by block, favor by favor, body by body.

Seize your territory

Push your crew into contested blocks and bleed the old families dry. Every warehouse taken and racket controlled tightens your grip on the underworld.

Run the rackets

Protection, smuggling, data-laundering—every district hides an economy the corporations pretend not to see. Tap it, tax it, and keep the credits flowing.

Break your rivals

Twenty-four syndicates want your throne. Turn their lieutenants, burn their safehouses, and make an example the whole city can read in neon.
