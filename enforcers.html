FACTION PROFILE

The Enforcers

VIOLENCE. ORDER. CONTROL.

The Enforcers are the iron fist of corporate rule. Armed, armored, and loyal to the regime, they maintain control in the city’s lawless zones—by any means necessary.

BACKGROUND

Once part of a private military initiative, the Enforcers were absorbed by NeoNet’s security division after the Unification Wars. Now, they enforce corporate law where government power fades. They are judged not by justice, but by results.
